## Main adoption caveats

- Pure nature C15 remains very weak at half impact: 24.73% /18.75% under independent and 22.42% /14.38% under reservation. A fresh half-impact pure-material/demon counter pass alone does not make this distribution desirable.
- Zero with old independent AUTO drops pure nature to 8.56% /3.28%, whereas zero/reservation gives 44.16% /36.56%. The policy is therefore part of any proposed playtest condition.
- Original Janus must stay visible as a weak recommendation. The separately frozen five-tag comparison uses common opponents and cannot replace original fixed-counter failures.
- None of these row scores proves human balance or establishes a 50% target. Lower-cost nature, overlapping roles and fixed automatic policies limit the comparison. No new acceptance floor is invented after outcomes.
