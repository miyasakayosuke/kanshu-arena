# Fenrir motion candidate: visual review

Rendered source: `dcc02c4cbc42888136a333abf67af415f722f1d2`  
Associated CI run: `38565441938`

## Inspection

Viewed the original artwork, neutral/loaded/braced/recovered rendered keyframes, mobile screenshot, and 12 sequential samples extracted directly from the six-second MP4 at 0.5-second intervals. This was **frame-sequence inspection, not real-time playback inspection**. Findings concern visual quality, not merely test results.

## Findings

- Original identity is preserved well: face, textured fur, markings, and accessories remain recognizable, without conspicuous redraw or an invented mouth.
- The head-and-shoulder dip is visible, with paws remaining substantially planted and recovery returning close to neutral.
- Main weakness: the chest shortens, front legs bow, and rear leg stretches diagonally without convincing joint articulation. At full size, the motion has a soft, slightly rubbery compression quality.
- The visible foreleg remains essentially the original planted stance. Shape changes do not amount to a distinct step or deliberate foreleg action.
- The face remains recognizable but subtly compresses with the crouch. Its calm expression makes the action read more as bracing or a preparatory dip than a dramatic threat.
- Chain links remain recognizable, with no obvious break in the inspected samples. Their movement appears coupled to the leg deformation rather than independently weighted swinging.
- At approximately 130 px, silhouette, ears, tail, and stance remain legible. Fine facial and chain changes disappear; the body dip is the principal readable action.

## Conclusion

Worth showing as an **original-art crouch and brace work-in-progress** to judge direction and motion amplitude. It is **not game-ready**: convincing articulation, weight, and stronger dramatic intent remain unresolved. This review does **not** constitute owner acceptance or authorize production adoption.
