## All-family distribution caveat for any adoption discussion

At zero impact, pure nature C15 falls to 8.56% overall and 3.28% against C17 opponents under independent/old AUTO, compared with 44.16% overall and 36.56% against C17 under reservation. These remain cross-budget C15-versus-C17 comparisons. Removing the two hybrid shells’ dominance is not sufficient reason to recommend zero with old AUTO for the all-family roster. Original Janus C17 is also weak at 20.89% against C17 under zero/reservation, which motivates the separately frozen recommendation check rather than hiding the original recommendation’s weakness.

For pure nature at zero, independent → reservation changes executed poison casts 4.05 → 1.79, actual direct HP 594.67 → 815.75, tick HP inflicted 188.75 → 163.68, and deaths before a queued action 1.78 → 1.20 per game. These are actual stored resources, not inferred nominal damage. The score change accompanies a policy-dependent action/tempo shift.

Reservation changes selection utility using only public state. It does not promise that the first planned poison caster will survive or act. If that caster is killed before its turn, later casters may have declined poison based on a planned application that never happened. Complete action-cancellation and poison-application ledgers are retained; the reservation policy is a separate arm, not a universally superior policy or a production default.
