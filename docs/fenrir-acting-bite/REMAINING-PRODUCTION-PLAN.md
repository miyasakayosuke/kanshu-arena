## Method assessment

Adjacent-conditioned whole-body drawings have produced useful coarse changes:
apex aim, downward articulation, jaw closure, forepaw reach, compression, rear
landing and rise. They preserve broad identity better than independent grid
images. The two local handoff edits expose a different limit: the requested small
toe change became an80source-pixel retraction, and contact toe/chain constraints
were not obeyed. Do not repeat such generations indefinitely or compensate with
sprite offsets. Source-level precision cleanup needs a controlled method that
actually preserves the unaffected pixels/landmarks. No external service or artist
is authorized, and no alternate editing method is adopted by this note.
