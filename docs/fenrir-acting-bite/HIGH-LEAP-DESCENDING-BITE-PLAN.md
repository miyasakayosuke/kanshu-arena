# High leap and descending bite: choreography clarification

Date: 2026-10-15 UTC. Status: storyboard and bounded next experiment only.

The requested acting direction is now a clearly higher leap with a bite during
descent: deep crouch, forceful hind-leg push, a short apex/aim, diagonal-down
jaw-led attack, then a heavy landing. This supersedes the earlier horizontal
lunge emphasis for the same Moonlit Bite. Keep the sequential landing comparison
and its defects; do not discard it or call the old airborne phase final.

## Fixed action clock

A feasible first timing study, to verify visually rather than treat as final:
- 0–100 ms: exact original neutral.
- 100–260: deep hind-loaded crouch, head lowered with eyes still on the target.
- 260–400: planted hind-leg extension and toe-off; forelegs gather upward.
- 400–500: visible upward flight with folded legs and tail/chain lag.
- 500–560: short apex aim; four limbs tucked/readable, head begins aiming down.
- 560–730: accelerate downward; head/jaw lead, shoulders and hips follow, legs
  prepare to reach ground. Draw the new anatomy/orientation, not a rotated image.
- 730–825: one bite; close at the existing800 ms hit and give it a short clear hold.
- 825–890: downward follow-through and forepaw reach, leading into contact.
- 890–1130: retained sequential contact/compression/rear-landing/rise experiment.
- 1130–1500: reviewed return keys and exact original endpoint, with the remaining
  head-turn/foot/detail continuity work still explicit.

Power55, MP10, one800 ms impact and total1500 ms are unchanged. There is enough
clock time for the proposal; visual legibility and continuity remain unproven.
Do not reschedule game events to make the animation look right.

## Spatial feasibility must be checked first

The exact accepted main14134f351d8eca79714199e1f2b34dc76e826a4e source was read:
`src/style.css` gives the short-screen battleScene a140–196 CSSpx height and
hidden overflow. `src/enemyFormation.ts` already height-limits the formation and
reserves an upper margin. The isolated130/142px diagnostic is not proof that a
new high leap fits every real battle scene.

First test a genuine airborne foot clearance around0.5–0.6 original sprite widths
(about65–85 CSSpx at130/142), using a compact tucked apex pose, while preserving
physical character scale. This is a design target, not an accepted measured jump.
Use no camera motion or effects. Record alpha bounds at every sampled time in a
review stage with headroom, plus an explicit140px/196px short-stage fit test.
If the true high leap cannot fit the short scene, report the required presentation
space/staging accommodation. Do not silently shrink the wolf, crop the apex,
warp the limbs or claim the taller diagnostic proves game integration.

Keep the landing support point coherent. Prefer a mainly vertical body trajectory
with a drawn forward/down head/shoulder reach inside the body envelope. Do not
move the held bite backward or reset an offset instantly to recover the old
landing anchor. Any forward-displaced landing would require a real back-step
sequence, which is not supplied by a coordinate trick.

## Small next art gate, before full airborne production

Create only two defining whole-body drawings initially:
1. Compact apex aim: clearly airborne, all four legs anatomically folded, adult
   head/chest/paw masses, focused downward gaze, coherent tail and chain lag.
2. Descending bite preparation: adjacent-frame-conditioned from the apex and the
   original model, with head/jaw aimed downward and forward, shoulders following,
   hips higher and forelegs preparing their reach. This must read as a diving
   monster, not the neutral wolf rotated as one flat card.

Use the successful full-height, adjacent-endpoint-conditioned process, stable
physical scale and shared model landmarks. Keep cuffs on the same limbs, one
attached chain with an open broken terminal and four readable legs. Inspect the
pair at130/142px and enlarged, then test the actual timed apex/descent path and
short-stage bounds before filling takeoff, jaw closure or reach in-betweens.
If the pair loses identity/anatomy or cannot read at native size, retain the result
and stop that experiment for a method/staging decision rather than regenerate an
unbounded sheet. No external animator contact, service, fee or upload is part of
this plan. Only the built-in image tool is used for requested new artwork.
