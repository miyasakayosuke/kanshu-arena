## Important asymmetries retained in review

Pure nature’s 44.16% overall row should not conceal its 0–12.5% scores against the recommended beast, dragon, material, slime and demon C17 teams. It scores 56.25% against pure undead. Among existing lower-cost opponents, it scores 100.00% against old-triple-poison C15 and 84.38% against Janus-poison C15. This is strong matchup polarity and a budget caveat, not evidence that all nature play is uniformly viable.

Pure demon is strong overall and in the fresh nature confirmation, but has retained losing matrix cells: 37.50% versus material-break-mixed, 46.88% versus pure dragon, and 46.88% versus slime-strong-poison. These small exploratory cells do not establish hard or universal counters.

No extra games, source changes, new floor, counter replacement or outcome-driven extension were added for this view. The broader distribution and resource costs, fresh 128-seed pure confirmation, original failed mixed probes and separate Janus recommendation study remain separate pieces of evidence.
